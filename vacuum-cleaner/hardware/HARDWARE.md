# Hardware Used

* **Arduino Uno** — main microcontroller

* **Arduino Motor Shield** — controls all 4 motors without needing separate motor driver ICs

* **4x DC Motors & Wheels**

* **Servo Motor (MG90S)** — rotates the ultrasonic sensor to scan for obstacles

* **Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04)** — detects distance/obstacles

* **Fan with its Motor** — creates suction for vacuuming

* **Battery Pack** — powers the Arduino/system
